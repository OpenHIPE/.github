OpenHIPE is an open-source ecosystem for evaluating XR and robotic systems with people in mind. We build shared tools to capture sensor data, run repeatable experiments, and share results across research communities.

## Our vision

AI is giving machines new ways to see and act in the world. XR headsets and robots can now sense their surroundings, move through them, and work side by side with people. We call these *interactive perception systems*.

Because these systems work with people, algorithm benchmarks alone can't tell us if they succeed. We also need to know how perception errors affect users, how people adapt, and whether the experience stays useful and safe. Today, the tools for these studies are scattered across devices, vendors, and labs, so results are hard to reproduce or compare.

OpenHIPE brings these pieces together. We provide open tools to capture and replay sensor data, run the same experiment across platforms, and share data and results. We also want to grow a community that builds shared evaluation practices together. We are starting with XR and robotics. Read the full [vision article](https://openhipe.github.io/vision).
